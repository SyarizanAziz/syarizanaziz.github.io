Teaching anyone to find website problems with session replays.

The problem

Web analytics tell you what visitors do: how long they stay, where they leave, whether they submit a form. They rarely tell you why. Session replays show the why, because you watch real visits play out. But watching recordings without a method is slow, and two people watching the same recordings often come back with different conclusions.

The module needed a way for anyone at the agency, not only analysts, to review replays the same way and come back with findings a team could act on.

The method I taught

1

Pick a segment

Filter recordings by entry page, pages visited and date range, so every finding is about one kind of visit.

2

Log every recording

Record device, entry and exit page, visit length, bounce and form submission in the shared template.

3

Tag the problem

Mark each issue as technical (broken or slow) or non-technical (confusing or unclear), so it reaches the right team.

4

Back it with proof

Write each insight in one line and link a sample recording that shows it happening.

Findings from the model answer

The worked example analysed a B2B agency's marketing website over six months. Each segment starts from a different entry page.

Entry: /about3 sessions
  • TechnicalThe Contact Us button was plain text with no link, so clicks went nowhere.
  • Non-technicalVisitors looked for the office address instead of filling in the form.
Entry: /services6 sessions
  • TechnicalThe Services page and homepage loaded slowly.
  • Non-technicalVisitors spent most of their time on the Services page.
Entry: /homepage7 sessions
  • TechnicalThe contact form did not load on desktop and the loading icon kept spinning.
  • Non-technicalOnly 1 visitor filled in the form. Most used the address and email instead.
Entry: /contact8 sessions
  • TechnicalThe contact form did not load on mobile, and the page was slow.
  • Non-technicalVisitors spent more time on the homepage, About and Services than on the Contact page.

What I took away

Proof beats opinion

An insight is easier to act on when there is a recording that shows it happening.

Split technical from behaviour

A broken form goes to developers. An unclear offer goes to content. Tagging early sends each issue to the right place.

Small samples still help

A handful of sessions per segment was enough to find broken forms and slow pages. Bigger samples are needed before judging behaviour trends.
